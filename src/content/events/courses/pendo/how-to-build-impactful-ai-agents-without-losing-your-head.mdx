This course covered five core principles for building AI products when the
technology, and the pressure to ship something with AI in it, moves faster than
anyone can track. It set out the questions worth answering before choosing a
model or writing a spec, covering the outcome being driven, the customer problem
standing in the way, its value, business viability, and how usable the
experience would actually be. The recurring theme was that AI is the tool rather
than the brief, and that it accelerates the product fundamentals instead of
replacing them.
