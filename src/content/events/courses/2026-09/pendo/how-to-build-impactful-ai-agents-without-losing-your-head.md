This course covered five core principles for building AI products when the
technology and the pressure to ship something with AI in it move faster than
anyone can keep up with. It set out the questions worth answering before
choosing a model or writing a spec, covering the desired outcome, the customer
problem standing in the way, its value, business viability, and how usable the
experience would actually be. The recurring theme was that AI is a tool rather
than the brief, and that it accelerates product fundamentals rather than
replacing them.
