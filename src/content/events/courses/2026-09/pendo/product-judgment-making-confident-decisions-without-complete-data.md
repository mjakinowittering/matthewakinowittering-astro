This course explored how to make high-quality product decisions when data is
incomplete, stakeholders disagree, and certainty never arrives. Built on Mike
Belsito's "Judgment in the Fog" talk, it introduced three habits: capturing the
signal by combining quantitative and qualitative data, finding the real problem
behind a stakeholder's ask, and picking the upstream problem that makes others
easier to solve. It also covered the "Or Maybe" stress test for challenging
problem statements before committing.
