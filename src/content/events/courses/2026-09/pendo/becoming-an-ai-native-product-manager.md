This course explored how the product operating model is shifting as AI shortens
the loop from idea to shipped software, making speed of iteration the measure
that matters. It covered using open-source repositories and agent skills to scan
the market, building small tools and AI skills to address everyday workplace
friction, and coordinating multiple agents through a shared ledger. It closed on
instrumenting what is built to prove its impact through real usage.
